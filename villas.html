Property Types

Villas, condos and land.

Villas for sale and rent

Private villas are the heart of the Koh Samui market, from beachfront homes in Choeng Mon to hillside sea-view villas in Chaweng Noi and Taling Ngam. Most have a private pool, and many suit both owner use and holiday letting.

Condos and apartments

Condominiums offer a lower entry price and simpler upkeep, often in managed developments with shared pools and security. Foreign buyers can generally hold a condo in their own name within the foreign ownership quota, which a lawyer can confirm for each building.

Land

Land suits buyers planning to build. Thai law limits foreign ownership of land, so buyers usually look at long leases or a structure set up with legal advice. Check access, utilities, title and building rules before you commit.

How to Buy

Buying a property in Thailand, step by step.

A general overview only. Always take independent legal advice before you pay anything.

1. Plan

  • Set your budget, including fees and taxes
  • Decide how you will use the property
  • Choose an area and property type

2. Search and shortlist

  • Tell us your brief and we will shortlist
  • View in person or on a live video tour
  • Compare ownership structures

3. Due diligence

  • Hire an independent lawyer
  • Check title, permits and any debts on the property
  • Confirm what is included in the sale

4. Reserve

  • Sign a reservation agreement
  • Pay a reservation deposit only against a written agreement
  • Agree the timeline in writing

5. Contract

  • Your lawyer reviews the sale and purchase agreement
  • Agree payment stages
  • Arrange the transfer of funds

6. Transfer

  • Registration at the Land Office
  • Pay transfer fees and taxes as agreed
  • Receive your documents and keys
