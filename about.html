Our Story

Built on the island, for owners abroad.

Samui Luxury Stays began with a frustration our founders knew first-hand: that managing a beautiful home from thousands of miles away too often meant patchy communication, surprise costs, and care that fell short of the property itself.

We set out to be the company we wished existed — resident on Koh Samui, small enough to stay personal, and disciplined enough to meet the standards of a five-star resort. Today we look after a select portfolio of distinguished villas for owners across Europe, Asia and beyond.

We deliberately limit how many homes we take on. It's the only way to guarantee that every owner gets a manager who truly knows their property — and answers the phone themselves.
