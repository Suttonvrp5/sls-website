Home  /  Relocation

Find your home on Samui, before you arrive.

Moving to the island but not here yet? Our team views, vets and shortlists rental properties on your behalf — so your first day on Koh Samui starts with keys, not a hotel room and a browser tab.

The Problem

House-hunting from another country rarely works.

Photos flatter. Listings go stale. Landlords disappear. Without someone on the ground, you're relying on strangers and guesswork for one of the biggest decisions of your move.

How It Works

Four steps, handled for you.

1

Tell us your brief

Budget, area, move-in date, bedrooms, must-haves and deal-breakers — a short call or form is all we need.

2

We search & shortlist

Our team pulls from local listings, agents and off-market contacts most overseas renters never see.

3

We view & vet in person

Every shortlisted property is physically inspected — condition, noise, water pressure, internet, the lot — with photos and video sent to you.

4

You choose, we arrange

You pick from a trusted shortlist. We can also help negotiate terms and coordinate the handover before you land.
